\chapter{OBJECTIVES}\label{objectives}

\section{General}

Develop and validate a three-degree-of-freedom parallel ankle rehabilitation robot 
(PRANK) with an RRR electric motor configuration, implementing admittance-based 
control and a mixed reality serious game to deliver adaptive, patient-responsive 
therapy, and demonstrating the system's ROM measurement accuracy through comparison 
with an optoelectronic motion capture reference.

\section{Specific Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
    \item Design, fabricate, and assemble a three-degree-of-freedom parallel robotic structure actuated by an RRR electric motor configuration, 
    integrating force and motion sensing to enable safe and controlled ankle rehabilitation movements.
    \item Develop an admittance-based control system with adaptive resistance modes—passive, assistive, and resistive—that adjusts therapy 
    difficulty in real time based on ankle ROM and user-applied force, complemented by a mixed reality serious game that provides interactive feedback and promotes patient engagement.
    \item Validate the system's ROM measurement accuracy through a pilot study with healthy subjects, comparing robot-estimated ankle kinematics
     against a reference optoelectronic motion capture system to demonstrate feasibility for clinical biomechanical assessment.
\end{itemize}
