\chapter{SCHEDULE}\label{schedule}
\graphicspath{{images/}}

The development of the PRANK ankle rehabilitation robot is structured into the five stages described in the Materials and Methods chapter.
The project timeline spans \textbf{7 months} (2026), with parallel execution of independent workstreams where feasible to maximise efficiency within the available time, as illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:gantt-prank}.

The schedule is organised as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 — Mechanical Design and Fabrication (Months 1--3):}
    3D design in SolidWorks, structural FEA, kinematic workspace verification via SolidWorks Motion, component procurement, and physical assembly of the parallel platform.

    \item \textbf{Stage 2 — Electronic Integration and Sensing (Months 2--4):}
    Selection and procurement of BLDC motors, encoders, motor drivers, six-axis load cell, and STM32 board.
    PCB layout for signal conditioning, wiring, dual-core firmware architecture setup (FreeRTOS, CAN bus, ADC), and sensor calibration.
    This stage begins in parallel with mechanical design once component specifications are fixed.

    \item \textbf{Stage 3 — Control System Implementation (Months 3--5):}
    C/C++ implementation of the admittance control law on the STM32, passive/assistive/resistive mode logic, safety monitors, and experimental tuning of admittance parameters.
    H2 validation experiments (step-reference and sinusoidal tracking) are conducted at the end of this stage.

    \item \textbf{Stage 4 — Mixed Reality Interface Development (Months 2--6):}
    Unity/C\# development of the three arcade game archetypes (1D, 2D, 3D), USB serial integration with the STM32, progression logic, and therapist dashboard.
    This stage runs in parallel with hardware development and converges at system integration.

    \item \textbf{Stage 5 — System Integration and Experimental Validation (Months 6--7):}
    Full system assembly and end-to-end testing, H1 workspace validation, H3 ROM pilot study with optoelectronic reference, statistical analysis, and documentation of results.
\end{itemize}

Continuous \textbf{documentation} of design decisions, experimental results, and the final preproject report runs across all seven months.
The milestone \textit{Full System Ready} is placed at Month 6, marking the point at which all subsystems are integrated and operational, leaving Month 7 dedicated to validation experiments and report writing.
