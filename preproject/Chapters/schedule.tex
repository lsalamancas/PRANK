\chapter{SCHEDULE}\label{schedule}
\graphicspath{{images/}}

The development of PRANK Phase~1 is structured into the four stages described in the Materials and Methods chapter.
The project timeline spans \textbf{7 months} (2026), with parallel execution of independent workstreams where feasible to maximise efficiency within the available time, as illustrated in Figure~\ref{fig:gantt-prank}.

The schedule is organised as follows:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Stage 1 — Mechanical Design and CAD Modelling (Months 1--2):}
    3D design in SolidWorks, structural FEA, kinematic workspace verification via SolidWorks Motion, and URDF/SDF export for simulation import.

    \item \textbf{Stage 2 — Electronic Architecture Design (Months 2--3):}
    Component selection (BLDC motors, encoders, motor drivers, six-axis load cell, STM32 board), schematic capture, PCB layout, bill of materials, and dual-core firmware architecture specification (FreeRTOS, CAN bus, ADC).
    The STM32 development board is configured and the firmware skeleton deployed during this stage.

    \item \textbf{Stage 3 — Embedded Control Implementation (Months 3--5):}
    C/C++ implementation of the admittance control law on the STM32 (passive/assistive/resistive modes), FreeRTOS task partitioning, safety monitors, and USB serial communication with the ROS~2 simulation host.
    The ROS~2/Gazebo simulation environment is configured with the URDF model and the HIL bridge node developed in parallel.

    \item \textbf{Stage 4 — HIL Validation and Performance Evaluation (Months 5--7):}
    Full HIL loop commissioning, step-reference and sinusoidal tracking experiments, H1 workspace verification in simulation, H2 controller performance evaluation, and H3 HIL fidelity assessment.
    Statistical analysis in MATLAB/Python and final report writing.
\end{itemize}

Continuous \textbf{documentation} of design decisions, experimental results, and the final preproject report runs across all seven months.
The milestone \textit{HIL Loop Ready} is placed at Month~5, marking the point at which the STM32 firmware and the ROS~2/Gazebo simulation are fully integrated and the validation experiments can begin.
